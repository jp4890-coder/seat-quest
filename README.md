# Seat Quest

An interactive HTML/CSS/JavaScript model of finding a seat on a NYC subway train.

Start at 116 St–Columbia University and travel six stops downtown toward South Ferry. Choose a waiting position, watch seated passengers leave, and see new passengers board.

## Run

Open `index.html` in a browser. No installation or external libraries are required.

## Model assumptions

- Six seats and three waiting positions represent a simplified carriage.
- Original seated passengers have hidden, randomly assigned exit stops.
- One to three passengers board at every stop.
- Seats are assigned to the nearest waiting passenger; ties are random.
- Passengers who obtain seats remain seated through the activity.
- Courtesy, accessibility priority, walking speed, and live service are not modeled.

## Project development

The student chose the phenomenon and directed revisions to the model and interface. ChatGPT assisted with computational thinking, pseudocode, and implementation.
