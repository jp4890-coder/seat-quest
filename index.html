Passenger guide & model rules

Board at 116 St–Columbia University and ride six stops downtown: Cathedral Pkwy (110 St), 103 St, 96 St, 86 St, 79 St, and 72 St. This activity ends at 72 St; the train continues toward South Ferry. Station order follows the MTA subway map. This is not live service.

Passengers have randomly assigned, hidden exit stops. Distance means how many aisle columns separate a passenger and a seat; both facing seats are equally near. The nearest passenger–seat pair is assigned first, with random tie-breaking. Seated winners remain until the end. At each stop, 1–3 new passengers board at randomly chosen waiting positions before seats are assigned. Their names continue from D onward. Everyone boarding wants a seat. Courtesy, accessibility needs, and walking speed are omitted. This is an invented simulation, not measured subway behavior.
